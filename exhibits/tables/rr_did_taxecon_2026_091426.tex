\begin{flushleft}\scriptsize
{\bf Notes:} Each cell is a separate difference-in-differences estimate with jurisdiction and period fixed effects; column (2) adds log GNI (GDP) per capita as a pre-treatment covariate; columns (3) and (4) repeat column (1) with both treated and control jurisdictions restricted to tax havens (union of the lists of \citeN{johannesen2014end}, the 2015 EU list and the sink offshore financial centres of \citeN{garcia2017uncovering}, EU havens included) and to all other jurisdictions (--: not reported; in Panel C, column (4), a single blacklisted non-haven jurisdiction is in the deposits sample). Panels A--B (RSIT ITI corporate tax rates, \citeN{wamser2025iti}) use the designs of Figure \ref{fig:ctr}: \emph{reviewed} compares jurisdictions selected for the 2017 screening with non-EU jurisdictions never reviewed (treatment from 2017); \emph{first greylisting} and \emph{first blacklisting} are estimated among reviewed jurisdictions, with not-yet or never listed reviewed jurisdictions as controls; column (1) therefore reproduces the ATTs shown in Figure \ref{fig:ctr}. Panels C--E extend Table 5 of the submitted version with the BIS Locational Banking Statistics vintage of September 2026 (to 2026q1), the UNCTAD FDI vintage of August 2026 (to 2024) and EU list revisions through 17 February 2026; \emph{first greylisting} excludes jurisdictions only ever blacklisted, \emph{first blacklisting} excludes jurisdictions only ever greylisted, and controls are all never-listed jurisdictions. Because almost every listed jurisdiction was reviewed first, these estimates combine review and listing; they are read as listing effects on the premise that review alone did not move deposits or FDI, and no review design is estimated for the economic outcomes; deposits are winsorised at the 99th percentile and FDI at the 99th percentile within year (missing values left missing). Tax rates and FDI are measured as fractions, so a coefficient of 0.01 is one percentage point (of the tax rate, or of GNI). TWFE and Gardner (2021) use an absorbing indicator equal to one from the first treated period onward; Callaway--Sant'Anna (CSA) is doubly robust.  Standard errors clustered by jurisdiction; CSA standard errors from a wild bootstrap. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.
