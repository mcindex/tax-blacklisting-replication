Trinidad and Tobago: Davendranath Tancoo, Minister of Finance
& 24 February 2026; statement to the Senate on removal from the EU list$^{a}$ (\href{https://www.finance.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MINISTERS-STATEMENT-ON-THE-REMOVAL-OF-TRINIDAD-AND-TOBAGO-FROM-THE-EUROPEAN-UNION-LIST-OF-NON-COOPERATIVE-JURISDICTIONS-FOR-TAX-PURPOSES.pdf}{source})
& ``The European Union's list of non-cooperative jurisdictions is not some harmless administrative label \ldots\ being placed on that list came with real consequences, serious consequences. It hurt investor confidence. It made financial transactions with European institutions by businesses and financial institutions more difficult and costly. It increased due diligence requirements for our domestic financial sector. It exposed us to the risk of defensive measures by EU Member States. And it damaged this country's international reputation.'' \\
